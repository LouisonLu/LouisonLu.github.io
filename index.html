PwP: Panoramas without Panoramas

Camera-Controlled 360° Video from Any View via Perspective-Only Adaptation

Louison Lu*, Qi Pan*, Ting-Hsuan Chen*, Hengyuan Zhang, Xinyu Huang, Cho-Ying Wu, Yue Wang, Liu Ren * Equal contribution

In collaboration with Bosch Research

First author, under review

Project page

PwP generates camera-controlled 360° videos from any initial view, from a single perspective image to a full panorama, without panoramic recordings in the target domain. It learns a panoramic prior from 360° videos and adapts it using perspective videos alone.

From one car camera covering 3.1% of the sphere, PwP returns the complete 360° video — and outperforms 360° baselines (Argus, ViewPoint, Imagine360) even from a single view.

Video diffusion, Cosmos Transfer 2.5, 360° geometry, point-cloud camera control, multi-GPU training

Heave-Ho: Decentralized Long-Horizon Multi-Humanoid Collaboration

Ting-Hsuan Chen*, Siheng Zhao*, Han Jiang*, Qi Pan, Louison Lu, Jiaheng Luo, Henghui Bao, Yiyu Chen, Quan Nguyen, Yue Wang * Equal contribution

arXiv 2026

Project pagearXivCode

A decentralized framework for collaborative multi-humanoid loco-manipulation. A post-trained humanoid tracking model coordinates physically coupled robots from local observations alone, while a vision-language planner and spoken leader–follower commands synchronize the team with no network communication.

Trained with two robots, it runs teams of up to ten, across 12 benchmark tasks, with no network messages.

My role: real-robot experiments — planning and running sim-to-real trials on Unitree G1 teams, diagnosing and replacing faulty hardware, and recording robot data.
